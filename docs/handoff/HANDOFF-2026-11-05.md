## 4. The air model (physics; on the session branch, NOT on `claude/physics-fixes`)

### What it is now

- **`src/sim/breathing.js` (new).** A crank-angle single-zone cylinder from EVO (blowdown)
  to IVC:
  - compressible orifice flow through both valves (Heywood App. C), curtain or throat;
  - lift profile sin^1.4;
  - seat events derived from duration at 0.050";
  - the intake runner as an implicit inertial column with Darcy friction and entrance loss;
  - a choke cap;
  - Woschni gas-exchange heat transfer (C1 6.18);
  - fresh and burned gas tracked separately;
  - the exhaust primary as a linear acoustic line (impedance a/A, collector reflection
    (A1−A2)/(A1+A2), valve-end reflection, attenuation);
  - `exhaustSystemDropKpa`: K·½ρu² through the manifold or headers, catalyst and cat-back.

  **One fitted constant: `BREATH_VALVE_CD` 0.52.**
- **`src/sim/throttle.js` (new).** Plate area (πD²/4)(1−cosψ/cosψ0) plus leak, Cd 0.8. The
  bore is sized for a 3 kPa drop at redline. Inlet duct K is 2.0 stock and 0.8 with the cold
  air intake. MAP comes from a bisection where the plate's flow equals the engine's demand.
  **A pull's `loadKpa` is a throttle opening** (`loadKpa/101.3`), not MAP.
- **`src/sim/airflow.js` (rewritten).**
  - `breathingGeometry(cfg, phase)`;
  - `tunedRunnerM`: the runner length that puts the torque proxy's peak on `runnerTuneRpm`
    (the published `crankTqRpm` for NA presets, 0.68 × redline otherwise);
  - `hardwareVeAt`: a lazily filled 100 rpm × 2 kPa grid with bilinear reads and 3 EMP passes
    (turbine back-pressure plus the system drop);
  - the head, fuel and DI factors and valve float are kept.
- **Removed:**
  - the empirical VE curve and `DEFAULT_VE` as a reference;
  - `baseBreathing`, `partLoadVeFactor` and `MOD_BONUS`;
  - the inlet Mach multiplier (`machVeMultiplier`), `charMultiplier` and `camPeakShiftRpm`;
  - the VE_EXHAUST_*, VE_BACKPRESSURE_*, OVERLAP_BACKFLOW_*, MACH_* VE terms and
    `CHAR_SCALE`.

  `inletMachIndex` stays as a yardstick only (engine.js doc says so).
- **Wiring.**
  - `solveInduction` takes `throttle` ({boreM, coldAirIntake}) and `exhaust`
    ({pipeDiaIn, mods}).
  - `evaluatePoint` and `simulateSweep` take `exhaustDia`.
  - `strategy.js` and `liveEcu.js` pass both.
  - The live steady MAP comes from `throttledMapKpa`.
  - The idle air is rescaled: calibration.js baseAir `max(2.7, 3.8 − t·0.0135)`, gains
    0.00135/0.00045, damp 0.018, and `IDLE_AIR_MIN_PCT 0.1`, `IDLE_AIR_MAX_PCT 15`,
    `IDLE_COAST_AIR_PCT 1.35`. Idle settles at about 800 rpm.
- **Sweep events.**
  - New `backpressure`: EMP/MAP ≥ 1.8 at ≥ 3000 rpm (`BACKPRESSURE_RATIO_LIMIT`).
  - `pastTable` now needs MAP above atmospheric.
  - The lean-target message quotes the point's own MAP, not the pull load.
- **Advisor (advisors.js).**
  - `FULL_THROTTLE_LOAD_KPA 95`, `PULL_CEILING_RPM 160`, `PULL_CEILING_KPA 4`;
  - `reachFull`;
  - turbo reach from the logged MAP plus slack;
  - `pullNear` for the measured ceiling;
  - `calibrationAdvice` takes `exhaustDia`.
- **Seeded live RNG.** `liveRandom(s)`, mulberry32 in `s.rng` (liveCore.js). False knock uses
  it.
- **Presets.**
  - NA presets carry `runnerTuneRpm = factory.crankTqRpm`.
  - The factory calibration prices the preset's own exhaust.
  - The VQ35DE and VQ35HR comments are rewritten for the new model.
- **Scoring and exhaust.**
  - The Engineer Score marks only an undersized pipe (more than 0.3" under the ideal), with
    "Exhaust pipe too small for the power: back-pressure costs the top end".
  - Oversized costs nothing, because in the physics it costs nothing.
  - `idealExhaustDiameter` is the on-screen target only.
- **Fixed this session.**
  - **NaN in the gas exchange**: with a 300° cam at 800 rpm, both valves drained more than the

## 5. Open (in priority order). These are real, measured, and still red where marked

1. **Light load at high speed** (red: physics "keeps every VE cell inside physically sane
   bounds").
   - What happens: stock VE falls under 10% at 20–25 kPa above 6000 rpm (5% clamp at 20 kPa
     6500+).
   - Trace evidence: at 25 kPa and 7500 rpm the cylinder reaches about 390 kPa at TDC on the
     exhaust stroke.
   - Cause: the derived seat events give the stock cam about 9° of seat overlap and an
     exhaust valve almost shut at TDC. sin^1.4 has no long low-lift ramps, so seat
     duration ≈ 0.050 duration + 38°, where real cams run +45–60°.
   - Next step: fix the lift profile or the event derivation in `breathing.js valveEvents` /
     `liftShare`, then refit `BREATH_VALVE_CD`. Expect this to also move the early NA peaks
     (item 2).
